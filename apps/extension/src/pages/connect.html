Linklog

Before Linklog reads your tabs

This extension shows your open tabs in Linklog's Browser view. It reads nothing until you accept.

If you accept, the extension:

  • Reads the address (URL) and title of every open tab, in every Chrome window, and your tab groups. The tab list stays in Chrome's memory until Chrome closes. The name, color and description of groups you link to Linklog, and which Linklog library linked them, stay on this device, so they can be linked again after a restart.
  • Sends the tabs of Linklog's window (URLs, titles, icons, groups) to the Linklog page when you open Browser view.
  • Only if you also allow page content: reads the text and preview of open pages. When a lens you turned on evaluates a tab, or when you auto-group tabs, its URL, title and text go to your Linklog library, whose server sends them to a remote AI provider for the answer. Auto-grouping also sends the names and descriptions of the groups you chose.
  • When you save a tab, sends only its URL. Linklog's server then fetches the page and extracts its content with a remote extraction service, like any link you add.

Never cookies, passwords or form fields. Your data isn't sold or used for ads. The providers we use are named in the privacy policy.

Connect Linklog to Chrome

Browser view shows the tabs and groups of the window where Linklog is open. Page text goes only to your own Linklog library, and only when an active lens needs it.

Tabs and tab groups

List the tabs of Linklog's window. Group, move and close only the tabs you select.

Allowed

Page content

Show a preview and the content of your open pages in Linklog, and let lenses read them. Previews (description and image) are read for every tab Linklog may access; preview images load from each site's own servers, like the page itself. Everything is read locally from what the tab already loaded — never cookies, passwords, form fields or history. Linklog reloads a tab (Load content) or puts it to sleep (Sleep) only when you ask. Page text leaves the browser only when a lens evaluates a tab or you auto-group tabs; saving a tab sends just its URL, which your library then fetches like any added link.

Chrome will describe this as “read and change all your data on all websites”. Linklog only reads.
