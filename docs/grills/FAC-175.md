# FAC-175: legal and user-data minimum for the first Unlisted submission (grilling decisions)

**Status: owner answered the questions (2026-11-26); pending final approval.** The answers are in [FAC-307.md § Owner answers](FAC-307.md#owner-answers-2026-11-26). This arena narrows FAC-175 to what blocks the first **Unlisted** Chrome Web Store submission and a small beta. Anything for a Public launch or for charging plans is listed as a follow-up and is not decided here. The privacy policy itself is in [FAC-307.md](FAC-307.md). The process is in [FAC-175-process.md](FAC-175-process.md).

**Not legal advice.** Every point marked **[LAWYER]** should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before a Public launch. Sources are primary and were read on **2026-11-26**. Anything not confirmed is listed under [No verificado](#no-verificado).

## Owner's words and fixed facts

- The task (2026-11-26): "lo mínimo legal y de datos de usuario que Linklog necesita para el primer envío Unlisted a la Chrome Web Store y una beta chica, y la política de privacidad publicada."

- Accepted research: [chrome-web-store.md](../research/chrome-web-store.md). The CWS listing goes Unlisted first, with the GitHub ZIP as the fallback, on Chrome only.

## Decisions

1. **Scope.** The minimum is six pieces before the Unlisted submission (D1 to D4, D7, D8), plus two before the first beta user who is not the owner (D5, D6). Everything else is a follow-up (see [Follow-ups](#follow-ups-not-decided-now)). The ticket's suggested full matrix (Terms, cookie banner, copyright, billing) was **rejected for this phase**. None of it is asked of an Unlisted item or a free beta, and criterion 3 decides.
2. **D1: the privacy policy at `https://linklog.facundopri.com/privacy`**, a static page in the app's assets. See [FAC-307.md](FAC-307.md).
3. **D2: FAC-305 option 1 is required by the policy, not an owner choice.** The background runs `chrome.tabs.query({})` when the worker starts (`apps/extension/src/background/index.ts:71`), before any click. The FAQ requires consent "before collecting or handling user data". Option 2 ("Connect is the consent") cannot meet "before". The owner still decides the wording and the look of the accept step.
4. **D3: fix the Connect copy in the same FAC-305 PR.** Two sentences in `connect.html` are false, and Connect is the in-product disclosure. (a) "Page text goes only to your own Linklog library": it also goes to TypeSafe (`jev.ts:317`). (b) "pages are never reloaded": `loader.ts:342` reloads sleeping tabs when the user asks to load them. Connect must also say that an active lens evaluates every eligible tab, including signed-in pages (`useLensRunner.ts:138`).
5. **D4: Privacy tab answers** (FAC-310): Personally identifiable information, Authentication information, Location (IP), Personal communications (saved social media posts, which the dashboard's own example names), Web history and Website content are ticked. Health, Financial and User activity are not. All 3 certifications are ticked. Remote code: No. The rule we follow: the boxes must match what the policy says Linklog collects (see process, swap on D1).
6. **D5: an operator account-deletion script before the first beta user.** Ley 25.326 art. 16 gives 5 business days, and today a deletion fails on a foreign-key error. It is a script plus a test, not an in-app feature.
7. **D6: one consent line on sign-up**, linked to the policy, before the first beta user. It gives the art. 6 notice before collection and the express consent of Decreto 1558/2001 art. 12 for the US providers. A policy page alone informs; it does not get consent. Whether a sentence or a checkbox counts as "express" is **[LAWYER]** before Public.
8. **D7: a policy contact address that works:** the owner's Gmail, the verified CWS contact (owner, 2026-11-26).
9. **D8: the Distribution tab excludes the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland** for the beta (owner, 2026-11-26). The policy says so. It is a checkbox in the first upload (FAC-313), with no code.
9. **Production triage and Limited Use.** No one reads what the extension sent (`browser_targets.url|title|capture_meta`, `jobs.result` of browser jobs, R2 `users/<id>/browser/`) without the user's consent for that data, an abuse or security investigation, a legal duty, or aggregated anonymized form. `triage-production` already asks for ids, counts and statuses. A one-line rule in that skill is a follow-up.
10. **The Slack paragraph** approved in FAC-307 goes into the policy in the PR that ships FAC-321, not before. Slack is not on `main` (only ADR 0004 exists).

### Rejected alternatives

- **Invite-only beta to avoid GDPR.** Sign-up is open and has no allowlist, so this needs new code (criterion 3). A Distribution-tab exclusion and sharing the Unlisted link only with testers cost nothing.
- **Policy on the landing.** The landing has no custom domain (`linklog-landing.<account>.workers.dev`, `/privacy` returns 404). A dashboard URL there would change when the landing moves.
- **Policy as a GitHub markdown file.** It is not on a Linklog website, and the Limited Use statement must be "on a website belonging to your extension".
- **Only Web history and Website content in the Privacy tab.** The tab certifies that the answers "reflect … your privacy policy", and our policy lists email, password and IP. Ticking less contradicts our own policy.
- **Terms now.** Neither CWS nor an Unlisted free beta asks for them. They come before Public or charging.

## Proposed changes to tickets (Linear untouched until the owner approves)

- **FAC-175** gets a new description: "Scoped to the Unlisted submission and the small beta (decisions in `docs/grills/FAC-175.md`). Public and paid launch items are follow-ups." It is Done when the owner accepts this doc and the tickets below exist.
- **FAC-305**: remove "Owner decision needed first: pick one". Option 1 is required, and the owner decides only the copy. Add these acceptance criteria:
  - A fresh install of the packaged build makes no `chrome.tabs.*` read and no bridge message with tab data before the accept action (prove it with `bun run test:browser` and a clean profile).
  - Connect names TypeSafe and Cloudflare as recipients of page text. It says an active lens evaluates every eligible tab, including signed-in pages, and no longer says "never reloaded" or "only to your own Linklog library".
  - Connect links to `/privacy`.
  - The extension version is raised.
- **FAC-313** (owner, first upload): in the Distribution tab, exclude every EEA country, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and keep visibility Unlisted. Acceptance: a screenshot of the saved region list in a ticket comment.
- **FAC-310**: Privacy tab answers as in D4, privacy policy URL `https://linklog.facundopri.com/privacy`, homepage `https://linklog.facundopri.com/`. Acceptance: every ticked box maps to a section of the published policy.
- **FAC-307**: see [FAC-307.md](FAC-307.md). It also takes the sign-up consent line (D6), so the page and its links ship in one PR.
- **New: "Admin: delete a user's account and all their data"** (blocks the first beta user who is not the owner).
  - A script `scripts/delete-account.ts <email>` deletes, in order, the user's `browser_targets` (the only foreign key that blocks; round 3 ran this order on a real e2e state with no FK error), then the user (D1 cascades requests, batches and the rest), then the `app_rate_limits` keys `writes:<id>` and `links:<id>`, then every R2 object under `users/<id>/`.
  - Acceptance: a test on local D1 with a user who has links, tags, lenses, a Browser evaluation, API keys and sessions leaves no row in any table with a user column or foreign key to that user, no `writes:`/`links:` counter for that id, 0 R2 keys under the prefix, an empty `PRAGMA foreign_key_check`, and another user's data untouched. Email-hash counters expire on their own within 2 days (the policy says so).
  - The runbook says "answer within 5 business days" and points to the policy.
- **FAC-306** already covers removing the localhost bridge from the store ZIP. Ship it in the same extension release as FAC-305 (one version bump): until then the packaged bridge also matches `http://localhost/*` and `http://127.0.0.1/*`, and the policy sentence "the extension passes this data only to the Linklog page" is false for the ZIP.
